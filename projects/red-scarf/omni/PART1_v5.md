## Prompt

```
A 15-second, 16:9, hand-drawn 2D storybook animation made of three shots joined by hard cuts, with relaxed, unhurried comic timing. This is part 1 of a two-part silent comedy short, "The Long Red Scarf".

Reference images (only two):
- The first image is the character model sheet of the main character. Copy her design exactly in every shot: a very tall, lanky young woman with a sleepy, half-lidded deadpan face, a short copper-orange bob with blunt bangs, light freckles, a long charcoal-grey wool overcoat, black tights, chunky brown lace-up boots, a paper bag with a baguette tucked under her arm, and an extremely long, chunky, bright red hand-knitted scarf with fringed ends that hangs down past her knees.
- The second image is a style and setting reference only, not a shot to recreate. Match its look in every shot: dark-brown ink outlines, watercolor and gouache textures on visible paper grain, a soft hazy overcast palette of pale grey-teal, sage and ochre, autumn leaves drifting in the wind, a seaside hill town of pastel terraced houses, cobbled streets and iron lampposts.

Everything else in the scenes is up to you, as long as it stays in this same hand-drawn style and town. Keep backgrounds simple and hazy so she stands out; the red scarf is always the boldest color in the frame.

Pacing: each shot is one gag with room to breathe: a calm setup, one smooth move of the scarf, then a reaction beat before the cut. She always moves from left to right and never turns back. Her face stays deadpan the whole time. Her scarf does the helping, moving like a playful living thing.

[0-5s] Shot 1, the bird's nest. Low-angle side view under a big autumn tree with orange leaves on a quiet cobbled lane. On a branch above sits a small twig nest with two pale blue eggs and a brown sparrow. She strolls in from the left with long, lazy strides, scarf drifting behind her. A gust rocks the branch and the two eggs roll out of the nest and fall. Without looking up, she keeps walking; the end of her scarf swings up beneath the eggs like a soft hammock, catches them, and tosses them gently back into the nest, one after the other. The sparrow settles onto the eggs, relieved, as she strolls on.
[5-10s] Shot 2, the café. Side view of a small corner café with a red-and-white striped awning, little round tables and wicker chairs on the cobbles. A young waiter with curly black hair, a white shirt, black waistcoat and long white apron carries a tray with a white porcelain teapot and three teacups. As she strolls past, he trips on a cobblestone; the teapot and cups slide off the tray and float up into the air. Her scarf slides out underneath them like a second tray, catches every piece without spilling a drop and tips them back onto his tray, one by one. He freezes mid-stumble on one leg, staring after her, stunned, as she walks on.
[10-15s] Shot 3, the kite. Static wide shot of a small hilltop park above the harbor: a bare tree, a wooden bench, an iron railing, the grey sea and a lighthouse far behind. Hold on the twirling scarf until the end; do not throw it yet.

Style: hand-drawn 2D storybook animation, fluid traditional character animation with clear comedic timing and smear frames on fast moves; the same ink line work, watercolor textures and colors in every shot.

Audio: windy autumn ambience with one light sound per gag: a worried chirp and two tiny taps of eggs, a soft woolly whoosh, clinking china, then the whirring swish of the twirling scarf. No music. No dialogue and no voices.

Do not recreate the second image as a shot. Do not use 3D, CGI, photorealism or a glossy anime look. Do not change her face, hair, coat or scarf between shots. Do not add a second scarf or change the scarf's color, length or texture. Do not show readable text, letters, signs, logos or subtitles. Do not add balloons, newspapers or ladders. Do not add snow; it is still autumn. Do not let her turn around or walk from right to left. No camera shake.
```
